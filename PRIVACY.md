# Privacy policy

Cometsnap is a screenshot and screen recording tool that works entirely on your computer.

- This program will not transfer any information to other networked systems unless specifically requested
  by the user or the person installing or operating it, with one exception you can switch off: the update check below.
- **Update check:** once a day Cometsnap asks GitHub (api.github.com) whether a newer version is out, and reads
  that version's short "what's new" notes (raw.githubusercontent.com). The request carries only what any web
  request carries (your IP address) and the app's version number; nothing about you, your PC or your captures.
  Nothing is downloaded or installed until you click **Update now**. Turn it off in Settings › Updates.
- No accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting.
- Screenshots and recordings are saved only where you choose (by default `Pictures\Cometsnap`) and copied
  to your clipboard. They leave your computer only when you share them yourself, for example by pasting
  or dragging them into another app.
- Settings are stored locally (in `%APPDATA%\Cometsnap`, or next to the exe in portable mode).

The website [cometsnap.com](https://cometsnap.com) and GitHub, where you download Cometsnap, have their own policies.

Questions: [open an issue](https://github.com/mike-risky/cometsnap/issues).
