Cometsnap for macOS: first launch
=================================

1. Drag Cometsnap into the Applications folder.

2. Open it. This build is free and not signed with a paid Apple certificate,
   so macOS blocks it the first time:
   - macOS 15 Sequoia and newer: open System Settings > Privacy & Security,
     scroll down to "Cometsnap was blocked..." and click "Open Anyway",
     then confirm with your password.
   - macOS 14 Sonoma: right-click (or Control-click) Cometsnap in
     Applications, choose Open, then click Open.
   Power users can instead run in Terminal:
     xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Cometsnap.app

3. Allow screen access when asked (System Settings > Privacy & Security >
   Screen & System Audio Recording > Cometsnap on), then click
   "Quit & Reopen".

Shortcuts: Cmd+Shift+2 capture (Cmd+Shift+4 too if you accept the offer),
Cmd+Shift+7 video, Cmd+Shift+8 GIF. Press again to stop a recording.
Print Screen on a PC keyboard (F13) also captures.

After an update, macOS may ask for screen access again: switch Cometsnap
off and on in the same Settings page.
