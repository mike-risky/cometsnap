Cometsnap for macOS: install
============================

EASIEST: one line in Terminal, no security warnings
  Open Terminal (Applications > Utilities), paste this line, press Return:

    curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mike-risky/cometsnap/main/macos/install.sh | bash

  It downloads the latest Cometsnap, puts it in Applications and opens it.
  Run the same line again any time to update.

WITH THE DISK IMAGE (.dmg)
  1. Drag Cometsnap onto the Applications folder.
  2. Open Cometsnap from Applications. This free build is not signed with a
     paid Apple certificate yet, so macOS blocks it the first time:
     - macOS 15 Sequoia and newer: open System Settings > Privacy & Security,
       scroll down to "Cometsnap was blocked..." and click "Open Anyway",
       then confirm with your password.
     - macOS 14 Sonoma: right-click (or Control-click) Cometsnap in
       Applications, choose Open, then click Open.
  If you opened it straight from the disk image, Cometsnap offers to move
  itself into Applications. Say yes: macOS only remembers its screen
  permission there.

THEN, ONCE
  The welcome window walks you through the rest:
  - Screen access: click "Allow screen access", switch Cometsnap on in
    System Settings > Privacy & Security > Screen & System Audio Recording,
    then click "Quit & Reopen".
  - Screenshot keys: leave "Use Cmd+Shift+3, 4 and 5 for Cometsnap" ticked
    and the macOS screenshot tool steps aside.

Shortcuts: Cmd+Shift+4 area or window, Cmd+Shift+3 whole screen, Cmd+Shift+5
Cometsnap panel (with the takeover), Cmd+Shift+2 always. Cmd+Shift+7 video,
Cmd+Shift+8 GIF, press again to stop. Print Screen / F13 on PC keyboards.

After an update, macOS may ask for screen access again: switch Cometsnap
off and on in the same Settings page.
